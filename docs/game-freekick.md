# Free Kick

3D-perspective penalty kick game. Tap to aim, swipe to kick with curve.

## Features
- Camera behind ball with perspective projection (2D canvas with projected coords)
- Tap upper screen to place crosshair on goal
- Swipe up to kick — direction controls curve, speed controls power
- Defensive wall: 2+level players (max 7), jump when ball is kicked
- Goalkeeper AI: dives toward estimated trajectory, speed increases with level
- Ball physics: gravity, spin/curve from swipe, air drag, ground bounce
- Progressive difficulty: level increases every 3 goals
- 5 kicks per round, score tracking
- Particle effects on goals/saves/blocks
