How to play

Two fingers always move the camera. Spin with two fingers, pinch to zoom in and out. That never draws anything, whatever tool you have picked.

One finger does whatever the tool says. Pick Line and drag: dominoes follow your finger. Pick Look and one finger spins the camera; pick Move and one finger slides the whole table around.

The glowing shapes on the table show what your finger is about to do, before it happens. Rub Out, next to Select, drags over anything you want gone, and Undo takes back a whole line at a time.

Turning things round. Whatever you just put down gets a blue ring under it — drag the Turn slider above the buttons to spin it any way you like. Tap Select and then any domino or trick to move the blue ring onto that one instead. The ring lets go as soon as you pick something new to put down, so the slider is always turning the thing you are holding.

Tricks opens the toy drawer. Pick one and the drawer shuts again so you can see the whole table — turn it with the slider first if you like, then tap where you want it.

The gold ring marks the domino that falls first. Use First to move it, then hit GO.
