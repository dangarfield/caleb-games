Sixteen days at the plant shop

Dragonseed

Everybody in this valley keeps a dragon. You keep the plant shop. People come in and describe a plant, or describe a problem. You have to work out which pot they mean.

On your first night you find an egg in the cellar. It is warm, and it is far too big. Something out on the moor is looking for it. You have sixteen days to decide what to do.
