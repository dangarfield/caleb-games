Three taps to throw

1
Tap WIND UP (or anywhere on the lake) — everything goes slow-mo and your arm draws back.
2
Tap for POWER — the longer you wait, the further back the stone goes. Stop it on the white middle of the gold band.
3
Flick forward to THROW — wait for FLICK NOW! and touch down on the white centre line, then flick up. Dead centre is a perfect throw.
