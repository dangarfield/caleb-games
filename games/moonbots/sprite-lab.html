SPRITE LAB

Every placeholder asset in Moonbots, drawn from js/art.js. When the real sprite sheet arrives, swap the art in that one file and this page shows what changed. Sounds are in sound-lab.html, and the game itself is in index.html.

RESOURCE ICONS

A 12×12 pixel-art icon for each resource, drawn in js/art.js (TPL + SPRMAP) and shaded from one base colour. They are grouped by the chapter that first uses them.

WORKERS

Scrap bots: upgrades, merge levels, tourists and battery states. Aliens: the three colours, plus hungry.

DOORS

Arch doors take bots. Round doors take aliens. A coloured ring only takes an alien of that colour.

CARD FAMILIES

Title bar colours by building family.

LEVEL BACKGROUNDS

Each chapter has its own pattern, and every level has its own tint.

CENTRE BUILDS

The centre plot after each level of a chapter. Built parts are solid; the part being built fills in from the bottom and glows.

CHAPTER FINALES

The four frames of each end-of-chapter scene. The game animates between them.
