How to Play

Goal

The big number in the white circle is your target. Combine the 5 small numbers around the hex using + − × ÷ to reach it.

How

Tap a number → tap an operator → tap another number. The result replaces the second number. Keep going until your total matches the target.

Math goes left to right — no PEMDAS.
3 + 4 × 2 = 14, not 11.

Play Level

  • EASY — 2+ numbers
  • MEDIUM — 3+
  • HARD — 4+
  • EXPERT — all 5

Card Type

Classic — all four operations, bigger numbers.
Fun Ones — only + and −, numbers up to 12.

Difficulty ★

Randomly assigned per card. More stars = bigger numbers & trickier targets.
