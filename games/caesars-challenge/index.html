Caesar’s Challenge

🌿 S P Q R 🌿
100 levels of Roman numerals, from Latium to Rome
Read the carved tablet and tap your answer. I=1 V=5 X=10 L=50 C=100 D=500 M=1000.
Right answers strike gold sparks and lay a mosaic tile; a streak builds your laurel multiplier. Miss too many and the Senate sends you back to retry the level — never the whole campaign.
Touch: tap, and drag to scroll the road. Keyboard: 0-9 / I V X L C D M to type, Enter to answer, 1-4 to pick a card, H for a hint, Esc for the map.
