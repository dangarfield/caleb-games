Abilities

1Insight — Highlights all nearby books from the same category as your held book (15s cooldown)
2Sort — Sorts your held books by category, series, then volume (5s cooldown)
3Guide — Shows a floating arrow pointing to the correct shelf for your held book (10s cooldown)

Tips

  • Check the spine label (e.g. "BQ #3") to identify a book's series and volume
  • Each shelf row is for one series — books must go in the correct volume slot
  • You can take books back off shelves if you placed one wrong
  • You can carry up to 3 books at once
